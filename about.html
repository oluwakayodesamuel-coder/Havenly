Our story

Everyday cleaning, made easy.

Finding dependable cleaning help in Lagos should not mean chasing people, guessing prices or hoping it gets done properly. Havenly makes professional cleaning simple, reliable and accountable, for homes and for businesses.

Our vision

Make reliable cleaning easy to discover, book, schedule and repeat.

Our mission

Deliver convenient, consistent and accountable cleaning through a simple digital experience and standardised service operations.

Our name

“Haven” means a safe, calm place. Havenly brings that feeling to your home, car and workspace.

How we work

We start small and get it right

Havenly is piloting in six areas of Lagos with a small, trained team. Every service has a written checklist, every price is shown before you confirm, and every job is recorded from request to completion. We grow only when the quality holds.

  • A short, clear menu of services, not everything at once
  • Prices, inclusions and exclusions on every service page
  • A complaint route with a real person behind it
  • We only promise what we can really deliver
